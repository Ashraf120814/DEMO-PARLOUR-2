Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What is permanent makeup?

Permanent makeup uses fine pigment deposits to enhance features such as the brows, giving a natural, low-maintenance definition that lasts over time.

Is microblading right for me?

Most skin types suit microblading well. We'll assess your skin, brow goals, and history during your consultation to recommend the best technique for you.

How does the consultation work?

We discuss your goals, review your skin and brow history, and map a shape together before any treatment begins.

How is brow mapping performed?

Using facial landmarks and symmetry guides, we chart the ideal shape and arch specific to your bone structure.

How long does the appointment take?

A first session typically takes 2–2.5 hours, including consultation, mapping, and treatment.

What should I expect during healing?

Mild redness and darker pigment are normal for the first several days, softening to the final natural shade as healing completes.

What aftercare is required?

Keeping the area clean, dry, and protected from sun and moisture is essential — full guidance is provided after your appointment.

When is a touch-up needed?

Most clients return for a touch-up 4–6 weeks after their first session, then periodically to refresh color.

Can mature or sun-damaged skin affect pigment retention?

Skin condition can influence how pigment settles and fades. We'll discuss what to expect for your specific skin during consultation.

How do I prepare for my appointment?

Avoid sun exposure, retinol, and blood-thinning medication for several days prior — full pre-care instructions are sent ahead of your visit.

What happens if I have concerns about my results?

We welcome a follow-up conversation any time — your comfort and satisfaction guide every step of your care.

Healing With Care

Care for Your New Brows

Before Your Appointment

Avoid sun, retinol, and blood thinners for at least 48 hours prior.

Immediately After

Gently blot excess fluid; keep the area clean and completely dry.

During Healing

Avoid makeup, sweat, and swimming on the treated area for 7–10 days.

Follow-Up / Touch-Up

Schedule your touch-up 4–6 weeks after your initial session.
